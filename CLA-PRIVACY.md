## Purposes and legal bases

Where you are personally a party to the CLA, we process information necessary to establish and administer that agreement under GDPR Article 6(1)(b). We also process proportionate evidence to verify rights, prevent unauthorized licensing, administer organizational representatives and establish or defend claims under Article 6(1)(f), based on the legitimate interests of maintaining a reliable chain of permission. These interests must be balanced against contributors' rights. Where a specific legal record-keeping obligation applies, Article 6(1)(c) applies only to the information actually required by that obligation.

There is no marketing purpose and no bundled consent to unrelated processing. Refusal to provide information needed to identify a rights holder or record an agreement may prevent acceptance of a contribution; it does not remove any independently available rights under the public software license.

## Retention and security

Acceptance and rights records are retained while reasonably necessary to establish permissions for contributions that are maintained, distributed or licensed, and for applicable claim periods. This can exceed the time an account remains active. We review the need for retention periodically, restrict access and remove redundant information. No fixed universal limitation period is asserted here. GitHub username changes and account deletion do not automatically eliminate the need for lawful evidence retention.

Exports containing personal information are kept privately with appropriate access control and backup protection. Legal terms may be public; individual contact details and authorization records are not published as an open signatures database.

## Your rights

Subject to the applicable conditions, you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction or portability, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. Erasure is not absolute where information is still lawfully needed for a contract or claims. Contact the controller using the address above. You may complain to a competent supervisory authority, including the President of the Personal Data Protection Office in Poland (Prezes UODO).

CLA checks are an administrative mechanism, not a conclusive determination of authorship or legal rights. Contact the maintainer for human review of an incorrect result. This process is not intended to make solely automated decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects within GDPR Article 22.
