## What is collected and from where

When you accept the [SplotLabs Contribution Terms](https://github.com/splotlabs/.github/blob/main/CLA.md), the signing form asks for exactly three things:

- your full legal name,
- a contact email,
- the pull requests you already have open in SplotLabs projects that the acceptance covers.

The signing service adds your GitHub username and account identifier, the version of the terms you accepted and the time. The maintainer adds the pull request and commit references that an acceptance covers, and, where an employer or client owns the work, the authorization that person or organization provides privately.

The form does not ask for an identity-document number, a home address or marketing consent. Do not upload sensitive data or private contracts to public issues or pull requests.

A confirmation that you write as a comment on a pull request is public, like the rest of the pull request.

## Purposes and legal bases

Your acceptance of the terms is an agreement to which you are a party. Recording and administering it is necessary for that agreement under GDPR Article 6(1)(b). Proportionate evidence is also kept to verify who granted which rights, to prevent unauthorized licensing and to establish or defend claims under Article 6(1)(f), based on the legitimate interest in a reliable chain of permission. That interest is balanced against contributors' rights. Where a specific legal record-keeping obligation applies, Article 6(1)(c) applies only to the information that obligation requires.

There is no marketing purpose and no bundled consent to unrelated processing. If you do not provide the information needed to identify a rights holder or record an acceptance, the contribution may not be accepted. That does not remove any rights you have under a project's public license.

## Retention and security

Acceptance records are kept while they are reasonably needed to show the permission for contributions that are maintained, distributed or licensed, and for applicable claim periods. This can be longer than the time an account remains active. The need is reviewed periodically, access is restricted and redundant information is removed. No fixed universal limitation period is asserted here. A changed GitHub username or a deleted account does not by itself end the need to keep lawful evidence.

Exports containing personal information are kept privately with access control and backup protection. The terms are public; individual contact details and authorizations are not published as an open list of signers.

## Your rights

Subject to the applicable conditions, you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction or portability, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. Erasure is not absolute where information is still lawfully needed for an agreement or for claims. Contact the controller at the address above. For data held by the service operator, SAP's privacy statement gives cla_assistant@sap.com. You may complain to a competent supervisory authority, including the President of the Personal Data Protection Office in Poland (Prezes UODO).

A status check is an administrative mechanism, not a conclusive determination of authorship or legal rights. Contact the maintainer for human review of an incorrect result. This process is not intended to make solely automated decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects within GDPR Article 22.
