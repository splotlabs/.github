You accept an approved version of these terms on the CLA Assistant signing page, signed in with your own GitHub account. The service records your account, the name and email you enter, the pull requests you list, the version accepted and the time. The Maintainer may agree another recorded method with you. **Pull requests already open.** When you accept, you list the pull requests you already have open in covered projects. Acceptance licenses your Contributions in the listed pull requests as they stand at that moment. A pull request that you opened earlier and did not list is not licensed by your acceptance, even if its status check turns green. Neither is a pull request opened before these terms were published in its repository. Such a pull request is merged only after you confirm it, in a comment on it from your own account or by another recorded method.

**Later submissions.** After acceptance, each Contribution that you intentionally submit to a covered project while the accepted version is in force is licensed under MIT-0 when you submit it. Each submission is its own grant for material that exists at that time. These terms do not license work you have not submitted, and you may stop contributing at any time. If you do not want a submission licensed this way, do not submit it for inclusion.

**New versions.** A grant is governed by the version accepted when it was made. A new version applies only to Contributions submitted after you accept it. The MIT-0 text does not change between versions.

A green status check, a ticked box in a pull request and a link in a README are not acceptance, and they do not prove who owns a Contribution.
