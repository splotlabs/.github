Commercial operations outside those permissions require an alternative license from the relevant rights holder. Private infrastructure or absence of software distribution does not, by itself, turn a business use into a noncommercial use.

**The rule is not “every legal entity must pay in every situation.”** The actual public license has permissions and exceptions. No payment obligation is created merely by viewing this page, by being a company, or by requesting a quote.
