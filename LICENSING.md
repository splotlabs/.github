## Public software terms

The first-party software in repositories that expressly adopt it is offered under **PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0** (`PolyForm-Noncommercial-1.0.0`), subject to each repository's actual license scope and separately identified third-party terms.

PolyForm Noncommercial is a source-available license, not an OSI open-source license. Its permitted purposes, including its stated organizational permissions, govern. This page does not narrow them or amend the standard license text.

Commercial operations outside those permissions require an alternative license from the relevant rights holder. Typical examples requiring review include business encoding pipelines, commercial cloud or SaaS deployments, and inclusion of Splot code in commercial products. Private infrastructure or absence of software distribution does not, by itself, turn a business use into a noncommercial use.

**The rule is not “every legal entity must pay in every situation.”** The actual public license has permissions and exceptions. No payment obligation is created merely by viewing this page, by being a company, or by requesting a quote. A commercial license is granted only under an agreement that identifies its scope and terms.

No rights are claimed in a user's original video simply because Splot processes it. That does not excuse unlicensed use of the software or supply rights in someone else's content or patents.

## Contributions

Other people contribute under the [SplotLabs Contribution Terms](https://github.com/splotlabs/.github/blob/main/CLA.md), which apply the unmodified MIT-0 license to the contributor's own material. The contributor keeps the copyright.

MIT-0 is a public permission. Everyone who receives a contribution may reuse that contribution, including commercially, without a license from Bartosz. This is an accepted consequence of the model. It applies to the contributor's independent material, not to the project around it.

Because MIT-0 allows sublicensing, Bartosz can include accepted contributions in releases under the project's license, under commercial licenses and under other licenses later. A later change does not withdraw permissions already validly granted, under MIT-0 or under an earlier public license. It also does not relicense third-party material or material with unresolved rights.

A permission exists only where a rights holder actually granted it. A link to the terms, a ticked box in a pull request or a passing CI run is not a grant. MIT-0 contains no express patent license; known patent rights are handled as the contribution policy describes.

## Distribution and dependencies

Keep third-party specifications, libraries, media, fonts, generated data and assets under their applicable terms. Commercial licensing of Splot does not purchase or replace licenses for independent third-party components.
